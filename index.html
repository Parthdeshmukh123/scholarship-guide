      Avoid fake agents

      Applying for a government scholarship is free.
      • Type the portal address yourself. Official sites usually end in .gov.in.
      • Never pay anyone to guarantee a scholarship.
      • Never share your OTP or password, even with someone helping you.
      • Not sure? Show it to your teacher or college office first.

      Where to ask for help

      College office

      Ask about fee concession and getting your form verified.

      Class teacher

      Check notices and ask before you trust a forwarded link.

      A senior who applied

      Ask which step was confusing and what they would do differently.
